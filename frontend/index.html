BEHIND THE CHECK

Three perspectives. One assessment.

The model combines different signals from sampled faces.
It does not verify audio, identity, or the original source.

◉APPEARANCE

Does the face look unusual?

Looks at visual patterns in face crops, such as shape, edges and texture.

≋TEXTURE

Are there hidden patterns?

Examines fine image patterns that can be difficult to notice with the naked eye.

▥CONSISTENCY

Does it stay consistent?

Compares features across sampled faces to look for changes over time.

KNOW WHAT A RESULT MEANS

Helpful evidence.
Not a final verdict.

The supplied evaluation reports 78% accuracy on 300 test videos. It does not guarantee accuracy for your upload, especially when face detection differs from the evaluation.

In the test set, the model flagged 134 of 150 face swaps. It also flagged 50 of 150 real videos.
What makes a good video to analyze?

Use a short, clear clip with a visible face. Avoid very dark, heavily blurred or tiny faces. In free hosting mode, clips must be 60 seconds or shorter.

Why can analysis take a while?

The server decodes frames, finds faces and runs the model. Free hosting uses limited shared CPU resources, and the first request may also wait for the server to wake up.

What if the result looks wrong?

The model can make mistakes. Check the original source, compare with another copy, and seek independent evidence before making a claim.
